These assets are ready for owner review and have not been uploaded or
submitted to Figma:

  Worldbend identity plus the current Perspective surface.

- `worldbend-icon-128.png` — 128 x 128 plugin icon.
- `worldbend-perspective-demo.mov` — 28.86-second H.264 recording of Free and
  Perspective corner editing; this was the owner's first-priority media item.
- `worldbend-transform-demo.mov` — 23.28-second H.264 recording of scale,
  skew, and rotation in Transform.

plugin using `examples/worldbend-demo-source.png`. Their frames contain only

three preview items, and the owner accepted their final presentation before
submitting the listing again on 2026-08-30.
